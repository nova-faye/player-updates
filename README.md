## Version 1.4.0

### New
- **Episode thumbnails:** on the home screen, the episode you stopped at now shows the exact frame where you left off, and all other episodes (finished or not yet watched) show a random frame from the episode.
- Thumbnails are saved on your computer, so they load quickly next time.

### Improvements
- Video playback now goes through a new internal system. This fixes the screenshot button (S) not working for some users.
- If the new system fails on a video, the app automatically falls back to the previous playback method.

### Notes
- Your history, playback positions and settings are kept.
- To update: Settings, then Check for updates, then Update now.
