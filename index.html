Features

Built for binge-watching

A clean screen

The interface is always hidden, even when you move the mouse. Press Space to show or hide it, and save screenshots with nothing in the way.

Folders become seasons

Pick a folder and every video inside shows up as an episode, sorted the right way (episode 2 before episode 10), subfolders included.

Pick up where you left off

The home screen shows your last episode with a thumbnail of the exact frame you stopped at, and what’s still unwatched in the folder.

Skip the intro

One key jumps past the opening. Set how many seconds to skip once and it’s remembered.

Themes you already know

Veyra, Dark, Light, Dracula, Nord, Solarized, Gruvbox and Catppuccin. Plain, familiar colors, and the video area always stays black.

Download

Get Veyra Player

For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The installer creates shortcuts and adds Veyra Player to “Open with” for video files.

  • Run the installer and follow the steps.
  • Open a folder of episodes, or double-click any video.
  • Later, update from inside the app: Settings, then Check for updates.
Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn’t code-signed yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
